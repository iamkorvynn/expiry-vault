## Short description (80 chars max)

Never miss a document expiry — PUC, insurance, licence, RC, passport.

## Long description

Your PUC, vehicle insurance, driving licence, RC and passport all expire
on different dates — and the fine for forgetting always arrives before the
reminder does.

ExpiryVault is a simple, private vault for every document that expires:

- Add any document with its number and expiry date, plus a photo of it
- See everything at a glance, sorted by expiry date, with clear status
  badges: Valid, Expiring Soon, or Expired — and a days-remaining counter
- Get reminded 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before each document expires,
  so you always have time to renew
- Mark a document renewed in one tap and keep its full renewal history
- Search and filter by document type or status

Your documents stay on your phone. ExpiryVault stores everything locally
— no account, no cloud, nothing uploaded. The expiry date is entered by
you, never guessed by a scanner, so reminders are always based on the
date printed on your document.

Stop paying late fines. Add your documents once, and let ExpiryVault
remember the dates for you.
